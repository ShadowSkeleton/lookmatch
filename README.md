# lookmatch

[![CI](https://github.com/ShadowSkeleton/lookmatch/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/ShadowSkeleton/lookmatch/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

Give lookmatch a photo whose colors you love, and it measures that look and
applies it to your own photos. You get a LUT and presets you can use in
Lightroom, Photoshop, DaVinci Resolve, and other editing apps.

lookmatch does not use AI to guess. It measures the colors in both photos and
computes the match, so the same photos always give the same result.

> **Status:** early development. Nothing works yet. Follow along or star the
> repo to hear when the first version is ready.
